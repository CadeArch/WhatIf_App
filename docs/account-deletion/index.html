MixedUp Account Deletion

MixedUp is published by Cade Rasmussen. This page explains how MixedUp users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

Delete Your Account In The App

  1. Open MixedUp.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm deletion.

When you delete your account in the app, MixedUp deletes your Firebase Authentication account and account profile data stored for MixedUp.

Data Deleted

Deleting your account removes:

  • Email address associated with your MixedUp account.
  • Username associated with your MixedUp account.
  • Profile progress, including games played.
  • Unlockable voice progress.
  • Account player record stored in Firebase.

Temporary room data is removed during normal room cleanup when games end or players leave.

Data That May Be Kept

Leaderboard entries or gameplay content that has already been submitted to shared leaderboard/gameplay records may be retained so existing leaderboard history does not break for other players. You can request removal of this data using the contact method below.

Some backup, log, or security records may remain for a limited period if required for security, fraud prevention, debugging, or legal compliance.

Request Deletion Without The App

If you cannot access the app, request account deletion by contacting the developer:

Email: rascade@gmail.com

Include the email address used for your MixedUp account so the developer can locate the correct account data.

Deletion requests are reviewed as soon as practical.
